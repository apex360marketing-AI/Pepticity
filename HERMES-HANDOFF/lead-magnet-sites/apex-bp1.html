    How it works · six steps
    I.

    Someone near you asks for a quote.

    A real person in your area, looking for your exact service, fills in a request on an APEX page for your industry.

    II.

    They agree to hear from you — by name.

    They give consent to be contacted by your business specifically. We record the time, page and wording, so you're covered.

    III.

    We check they're real.

    Phone and email are verified, and duplicates and fakes are removed before anything reaches you. [Your verification process]

    IV.

    We check they're serious.

    A few quick questions about the job, timing and budget. Every lead arrives with a score, so you know who to call first.

    V.

    It goes to one business: yours.

    Each industry in each area belongs to one business. Nobody else receives the same lead — it is exclusive to you.

    VI.

    It lands on your phone and in your CRM.

    A text and an email reach you within [target time], and the lead is added to your CRM with everything you need for the first call.
