02 How it works · 6 steps

From their requestto your phone.

  1. 0101

    Someone asks.

    A real person near you fills in a request for the service you offer — what they need and where.

  2. 0202

    Consent recorded.

    They agree to be contacted, and that agreement is saved with the lead. [Your consent wording]

  3. 0303

    Checked: real.

    We check that the person, the phone number and the request are genuine. [Your verification process]

  4. 0404

    Checked: serious.

    We check that they actually want the job done. [Your qualifying questions]

  5. 0505

    Matched to one business.

    The lead goes to one business in their area — you. It is not shared with your competitors.

  6. 0606

    On your phone and in your CRM.

    You get a text alert and the lead appears in your CRM, ready to call. [Supported CRMs]
