      How it works

      The route of a lead.

      Six stops between a customer asking for a quote and your phone lighting up.

        Stop I

        They ask for a quote.

        A homeowner or business in your area fills in a short request on an APEX page for your industry.

        NamePostal / ZIPPhone
        Stop II

        They agree to hear from you.

        Before sending, they agree to be contacted by your business by name. We save the exact wording and the time.

        "I agree to be contacted by [Your Business] about my request by phone, text or email."

        Stop III

        We check they're real.

        Phone and email are checked, and fakes, tests and duplicates are filtered out before you ever see them. [Your verification process]

        • Email address is valid checking
        • Not a duplicate or a test checking

        We find out if they're serious.
