Chapter i · How it works

Follow one lead down the trail.

Six stops between a person asking for help and your phone buzzing. Scroll, and the trail draws itself.

  1. 01The request

    Someone asks for help

    A real person near you asks for a quote, for example a roof repair or a new boiler.

  2. 02Consent

    They agree to be contacted

    We record who agreed, when, and to what, before anything is sent.

    [Your consent wording]
  3. 03Checked real

    We check they're real

    The phone number and contact details are checked, so you don't call a dead line.

    [Your verification process]
  4. 04Checked serious

    We check they're serious

    We confirm they actually want the work done, not just browsing.

    [Your qualifying questions]
  5. 05Matched

    Matched to one business

    The lead goes to one business in that area: you. It is not sold to a list of competitors.

  6. 06Delivered

    Sent to your phone and CRM

    You get a text, and the details land in your CRM, ready for you to call.

    [Typical delivery time]
