HOW IT'S MADE

  1. STATION 01 OF 05

    THE CHAIN IS BUILT

    Amino acids are joined one at a time, in an exact order, on tiny resin beads: solid-phase synthesis.

    Want the plain-words version in your inbox each month?

  2. STATION 02 OF 05

    PURIFICATION

    The raw chain is run through high-performance liquid chromatography, separating the target sequence from everything else.

  3. STATION 03 OF 05

    THE DROPPER FILLS THE VIAL

    The purified solution is measured into clean glass vials under controlled conditions.

  4. STATION 04 OF 05

    FREEZE-DRYING

    Lyophilisation removes the water at low temperature and pressure, leaving a dry, stable cake in the vial.

  5. STATION 05 OF 05

    SEALED AND CRIMPED

    A stopper goes in and an aluminium crimp seals it. The label carries the name and strength.
