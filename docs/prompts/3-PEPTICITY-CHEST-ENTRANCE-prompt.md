## THE SHOT, BEAT BY BEAT (9–10 s)
1. **0.0–1.5 s:** Darkness and low smoke. The case is big and close in frame (not far back), the camera slightly above and tilted so the lid's top is visible. The PEPTICITY logo is embossed on the lid top, catching a soft light.
2. **1.5–2.5 s:** A unique light accent wakes beneath the case: a thin line of light traces the base, then pools under it on the glass floor.
3. **2.5–5.5 s:** The lid opens slowly, with weight. Smoke spills over the edges, a little more than now.
4. **4.0–7.0 s:** A luminescent light ignites under the bottle, brighter than now. The bottle rises. The light carries up through the peptide swirl (the glass-bead chain), lighting each bead in turn.
5. **7.0–8.5 s:** Hero hold: bottle centred, swirl turning, smoke settling, light breathing.
6. **8.5–10 s:** PEPTICITY engraves itself across the front of the case, letter by letter, as if cut by light, in the bottle's silver (white version) or the matching trim (black version). It holds, then hands off into the hero.

## RECOMMENDATIONS TO MAKE IT EVEN COOLER
- **Light-cut engraving with sparks:** a tiny bright point travels along each letter leaving the engraving behind, with a few slow embers drifting off.
- **Pointer-reactive smoke:** after the film, the smoke parts gently around the visitor's pointer for a second before the hero takes over.
- **Lid reflection:** the inside of the lid is mirror-polished so the rising bottle and swirl reflect in it.
- **Seamless hero:** the last frame of the film becomes the hero background (the bottle keeps turning slowly in 3D), so there's no cut between the entrance and the site.
- **Two openings:** the film opens on a dark frame, so the first frame shows instantly; a skeleton of the hero loads underneath for speed.
