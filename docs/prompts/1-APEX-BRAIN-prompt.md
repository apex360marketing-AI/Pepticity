## RECOMMENDATIONS TO MAKE IT EVEN COOLER
- **Living growth replay**: a "watch your brain grow" button that replays every memory being added, sparks firing in order.
- **Hover a memory**: its tendril or line lights up end to end and a glass card floats out with the memory.
- **Search lights the path**: typing a search makes the matching memories glow and the rest dim.
- **Sound (off by default)**: soft synaptic clicks and a low hum that rises as the brain fills.
- **Brain ⇄ tree morph**: when switching, the brain's lines bend into tendrils and the shell becomes the canopy, instead of a cut.
- **Performance**: one GPU-instanced particle system for nodes; a still-image fallback for slow phones; reduced-motion version.
