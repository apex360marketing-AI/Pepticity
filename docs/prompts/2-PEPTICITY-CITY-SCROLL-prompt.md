## THE JOURNEY, BEAT BY BEAT
1. **Hand-off from the chest.** The PEPTICITY engraving on the chest flares and the light becomes the sky above the city (no hard cut).
2. **High above the city.** A white-and-gold city at dusk/night: ivory stone towers, gold-chrome edges, platinum crowns that shimmer. Warm white light only (the owner said extra colour looks cheap).
3. **The weave.** The camera banks and threads between the apartment towers: past balconies, around corners, under sky-bridges, descending in long elegant curves. Slow and luxurious, never a straight drop.
4. **Down low.** It levels out at street level, gliding toward one grand building, the PEPTICITY house, with the logo illuminated over the entrance.
5. **The doors.** Huge, hardcore, posh doors (top-secret vault meets luxury maison). Scroll stops here.
6. **The scanner.** A unique, highly animated, classy scanner. The visitor taps their card (the PEPTICITY member card): rings of light scan, the card authenticates, the locks release one by one, and the doors open with weight and light pouring out. **Lead hook:** first-time visitors get their card by entering an email ("Get your PEPTICITY card"); the tap uses it.
7. **The lab / office.** Inside, the peptides are being made right there: glass, chrome, soft studio light, the process in motion. The PEPTICITY logo is on the wall, illuminated, classy.
8. **The guided store (IKEA path).** The scroll keeps walking them past every offer and product in a set route: product displays with the bottles, the grand-opening offer, members price, restock alerts, the founding team. A hook at every stop, some floating in and fading as they pass. Classy, never cheesy. The route always ends at "Join the PEPTICITY Team".

- **The card is the lead magnet:** the email form produces a personalised PEPTICITY member card (their name engraved in 3D) that they then tap on the scanner. It turns sign-up into the key to the building.

- **Lab "made right here" moments:** each station (dropper, mixer, sealing, cold box) pins briefly with one classy caption.
- **Remember the visitor:** a returning visitor skips straight to the store with "Welcome back"; the journey stays one tap away.
