# Regulated-claims check: sample selling-machine copy (2 Nov 2026)

Risk scan, not legal advice. Run by the coordinator with the regulated-claims-check skill.
Rule driving it: hedged or research framing is evidence of intended use, not a shield (FDA Aug 2026 letters; Health Canada: "For Research Use Only" labels give no exemption). Delete the sentence; don't disclaim it.

## The ceiling
- For a product on Canada's Prescription Drug List (e.g., tirzepatide, HGH, tesamorelin): consumer advertising is limited to name, price and quantity (FDR C.01.044).
- For an unauthorized drug (most of the catalogue: BPC-157, TB-500, KPV, etc., named in Health Canada's advisories): there is no lawful claim layer at all. Any promotion that frames them for human use is the enforcement trigger (Canlab injunction, 2026).
- So every feature on this page is only as legal as the product it sells. The features amplify sales; for an unlawful SKU they also amplify exposure.

## Safe to say instead
- What it is (name, size), price in CAD, stock that is real, lot number and certificate (once real), shipping, returns, contact, order status.
- Store mechanics: cart saved, auto-ship cadence chosen by the buyer, points for access, gift cards.

## Counsel questions (these gate the work)
1. Can PEPTICITY lawfully sell and advertise each SKU in Canada (and ship to the US)? This gates every feature, the payment processor and the email/SMS platform.
2. Are price incentives (15% code, quantity breaks, subscribe-and-save discount, points, referral credit) allowed on any SKU that is a prescription drug or an unauthorized drug? (C.01.044 price-only rule; provincial inducement rules.)

## Prohibited-phrase audit list
heal, healing, recovery (as an outcome), repair, boost, burn, fat loss, lean, muscle, gains, performance, longevity, anti-aging, reverse, restore, support(s), promote(s), improve(s), enhance(s), optimize, may help, studied for, investigated for, explored for, protocol, cycle, stack, dose, dosing, dosage, daily, reconstitute, mix, inject, syringe, bac water, research use only, not for human consumption, clinically proven, doctor recommended, Health Canada approved/compliant, pharmaceutical grade, safe, no side effects, guaranteed, hurry, selling fast, only X left (unless real), X people viewing.
