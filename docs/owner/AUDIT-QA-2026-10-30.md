## RECOMMENDED (needs the owner's OK, with the reason)

- **Website load speed (carry into v3).** LCP is 8.5 s on a throttled phone (Slow 4G, 390 px), against a 2.5 s target, and the site pulls 3.7 MB in its first 30 s. Reasons:
  - the closed-chest poster is set by script only after all the page scripts download
  - three.js loads as the unminified build (250 KB here; the dashboards already use the 160 KB `.min` build)
  - the city and lab frame sheets start downloading 2.5 s in and compete with the film
  - Recommend: preload the poster for the chosen look from the head script; switch to `three.module.min.js` (and update its integrity hash); start the frame sheets only once the film is playing; serve the webm before the mp4 (BLACK film: 2.4 MB instead of 5.5 MB), once you've checked the quality matches
- **Website memory.** A full visit fetches and keeps every frame sheet decoded: desktop BLACK is 36 MB (city) + 26 MB (lab); phone BLACK is 14 MB + 8 MB. Each desktop sheet is about 33 MB in memory once decoded, which is a real crash risk on iPhones. Recommend keeping only the sheets near the scroll position. Biggest files: chest-white.mp4 7.4 MB, chest-black.mp4 5.5 MB, then each frame sheet at up to 800 KB.
- **The city starts black** until its first 600 to 800 KB sheet arrives (seen in testing). Recommend showing the doors photo underneath as a stand-in.
- **The concierge greeting covers the sign-up.** At 1440x900, in both WHITE and BLACK, the greeting card covers the right side of CLAIM MY 15% OFF and cuts off the end of the consent sentence. Recommend opening it lower or smaller, or only after the visitor scrolls. Screenshots: before_site_focus_d.png and before_site_white_d.png.
- **Client links open on Grace's view first.** /client/ and /dashboard-color/client/ start on the GRACE (OWNER) view and switch to CLIENT only after every file on the page has finished loading, plus 0.4 s. On a slow or blocked connection, a client sees Grace's studio first (in testing the switch arrived 39 s in, on a busy machine). Recommend switching as soon as the page is readable, or after 2.5 s at most. This is a behaviour change on a locked dashboard, so it needs your OK.
- **Contrast below WCAG AA:**
  - coin footer line "PEPTICITY · © 2026": #36383b on #0b0b0c = 1.67:1 (needs 4.5:1). This is the locked coin, so it's flagged only
