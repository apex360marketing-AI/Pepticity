## NEW SKILLS TO CREATE (gaps found)

1. **premium-restraint: the $30k look rulebook plus a "cheap detector".**
   - Accent budget: colour covers at most about 3–5% of the pixels on any screen, and only as edges, glints, key words and focus.
   - No tinted glass, no colour fills, no colour pools.
   - One hero moment per screen; generous space.
   - A script measures the accent coverage and the glass clarity on real screenshots and fails the page if it's over budget.
2. **clear-glass-spec: the exact recipe for fully see-through glass.**
   - Blur, a white sheen and hairline edges only, never a fill.
   - A pixel test checks that the glass shows the background through it.
3. **4d-direction: how every 3D object reacts.**
   - Pointer lean, scroll turn, idle breathing, light rake.
   - Motion budgets, 60 fps limits and a phone fallback.
   - A filmstrip proof of each object at 5 moments.
4. **lead-hook-proof: an end-to-end test of every hook on a page.**
   - Every form, quiz, call-back and exit card is tested.
   - Each one checks: CASL box unticked, the hook id is tagged, the success state shows with a next step.
   - Outputs a hook map with screenshots per screen.
5. **luxury-benchmark: a side-by-side of our page against 3 top award sites in the same niche.**
   - Uses apex-design-capture.
   - Scored for spacing, type, motion and restraint before the owner sees anything.
