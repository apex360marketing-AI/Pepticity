## WHERE THE LINE IS (read this first)

**The short version: we sell the store, the brand and the experience. We never say what a product does to a body.**

- **Why.** In Canada, a product that has no Health Canada approval can't be sold or advertised as something that treats, fixes or improves a health problem. Most of this catalogue has no approval.
- **Enforcement is real.** Health Canada named most of these peptides in a 2026 public warning. In June 2026 a Québec court shut down another online peptide seller (Canlab) for good.
- **"For research use only" does not protect us.** Health Canada says so. In August 2026 the US FDA sent warning letters to five "research" sellers. It used their dose charts, mixing instructions and bacteriostatic water sold beside the peptides as proof the products were really meant for people.
- **So the interest cards say what area a product class has been studied in.** Example: "researched in the context of cellular energy". That describes published science. It does not promise the visitor anything.
- **We never say:** treats, cures, fixes, boosts, heals, burns, builds, reverses, "helps with", "for pain", "for headaches", "for weight loss". No condition names next to a product.
- **No dosing, ever.** No amounts, no schedules, no "cycles", no mixing steps, no syringes, no calculators. That's the exact thing the FDA letters used.
- **No sexual-function anything.** PT-141 gets no interest card and no "what it's for" line. Same for anything about libido or intimacy.
- **No medical talk.** No doctor, clinician, pharmacy, prescription or consult wording (owner rule R055, R114) until those partners really exist.
- **Bacteriostatic water never sits beside the peptides.** Not on a shelf, not in a bundle, not as "you may also need".
- **The biggest risk is listing the products at all,** not the wording. Counsel must clear the catalogue before the store goes public. Until then the store runs in preview.
- **The 15% offer, member prices, restock alerts on peptides and referral credit** all sit behind one switch (`COUNSEL_CLEARED.offer`). When it's off, the same spots show the member card, Grace's letter or a news list.
- **Every number is real.** Stock counts, queue positions, reviews and countdowns come from real data or don't show. Fake urgency gets fined (Competition Bureau; one retailer paid $3.25M).
- **Every email and text needs a ticked box the visitor ticked themselves (CASL).** One box per purpose. Never pre-ticked.
- **US visitors:** same rules plus FDA, FTC, CAN-SPAM and TCPA (texts). HGH in the US is a federal crime risk outside a doctor's order. Recommendation: serve Canada only until counsel says otherwise.

## 8. TOP 15 RECOMMENDATIONS (ranked by sales impact)

1. **Hero offer card first.** Grand opening, 15%, one email field, GUEST or JOIN. The first screen decides most visits.
2. **All-in price in the cart drawer.** Shipping shown before checkout. Surprise costs are the #1 reason carts are dropped (39–48%, Baymard via secondary).
3. **Guest checkout, account after.** Forced accounts cause 19–26% of dropped carts. Offer "save your details" on the thank-you page.
4. **Save my cart + two cart emails (+1 h, +24 h).** Cart-based exit cards average ~17% vs ~1% for generic exit pop-ups (vendor data).
5. **Interest entry cards with one-field capture.** Turns "I need energy" into a shelf and a sign-up, with no claims.
6. **Restock queue with a real position and an instant email.** Back-in-stock is the best-converting automation in the research (6.7–8.3%).
7. **Welcome series that delivers the 15% with a real end date.** The code arrives in the inbox, not just on screen.
8. **3-tap catalogue finder with SAVE THIS SHORTLIST.** Helps the unsure buyer; quiz tools claim 7–25% (vendor, test it).
9. **Quantity breaks instead of goal bundles.** "3 OF THE SAME VIAL · SAVE [X]%". Raises order size without pairing products with a goal (counsel switch).
10. **Free-shipping line in the drawer, A/B tested.** One test showed order size +9.2% but conversion −5.9%. Set a real threshold `[$X]` and keep a hold-out.
11. **Lot passport, the day Grace has real certificates.** Trust matters most on expensive items. Never before it's real.
12. **Verified reviews after five.** Five reviews vs none lifted purchase likelihood ~270% (Spiegel 2017). No health outcomes in reviews.
13. **Refer a friend from the thank-you page and the dashboard.** Milestone programmes drove ~30% of new subscribers for Morning Brew (secondary).
14. **Welcome back + tab-title cue.** Returning visitors land on their saved cart instead of starting over.
15. **Thank-you page text opt-in.** Shipping updates (asked-for) plus a separate unticked "new lots" box: the highest-trust moment for a second consent.
