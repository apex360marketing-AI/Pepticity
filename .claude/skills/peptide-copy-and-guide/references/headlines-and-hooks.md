## 2. HOOKS (ads, social, email openers, hero eyebrows)
Category honesty is rare in this market, so it persuades. Each hook is about the process or the practice, never an outcome.
1. "Most peptide sites sell you a vial. We start with a clinician."
2. "If a site sells peptides 'for research', ask who checked they're right for you."
3. "The honest answer to 'will it work for me?' is: let's ask someone licensed."
4. "We'd rather tell you no than sell you the wrong thing."
5. "Your questions deserve a licensed answer, not a forum thread."
6. "Two minutes, a few honest questions, and a clinician takes it from there."
7. "Price you can see. People you can check. Answers that stay private."
8. "Curious about peptides? Start with the person who's allowed to say yes or no."
9. "No influencer protocols. No guesswork. A clinician who knows your history."
10. "Ask the questions you've been Googling, to someone licensed."
Rules: no fear-selling about health, no naming competitors, no "ban" or "loophole" talk, no scarcity, no testimonials until real and consented, no claims about the clinician's results.

## 3. SECTION PATTERNS (home and category pages)
1. **HERO**: intent headline, process subhead, one CTA ("START THE 2-MINUTE QUIZ"), one quiet line ("A licensed clinician decides if a plan is right for you.").
2. **HOW IT WORKS**: three steps: QUIZ → CLINICIAN → PHARMACY. Plain verbs. Timelines only when confirmed.
3. **WHO'S BEHIND IT**: clinicians (real, consented names and licences only, else `[Clinician network: confirm with Grace]`), the partner pharmacy, `[Your verification process]`.
4. **WHAT IT COSTS**: all-in price, what's included, taxes, what isn't charged if declined, cancellation.
5. **PRIVACY**: who sees answers, what is never shared, no ad tracking of health answers.
6. **QUESTIONS**: 6-8 from `qa-bank.md`, service-first.
7. **CLOSING CTA**: "BOOK YOUR CONSULT" / "START THE 2-MINUTE QUIZ", with "Prefer to talk it through? Call [phone number]."
Not allowed on these pages: product carousels, "best sellers", "popular stacks", product images in use, lab-coat staging.

## 5. SOCIAL AND EMAIL RULES
- Captions follow the same ceiling: no product names, no outcomes, no protocols, no before/after.
- Influencers and affiliates: written brief with the banned-phrase list; PEPTICITY is responsible for what they say; disclose the paid relationship; no affiliate commission tied to a named Rx product [counsel to confirm].
- Email subject lines are ads too: "Your PEPTICITY summary" is fine; "Your BPC-157 protocol" is not.
- Every marketing email: sender identified, unsubscribe working, sent only with the separate unticked consent.
