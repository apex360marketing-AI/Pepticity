# CHROME METAL POP

Owner (7 Nov): "It's still extraordinarily dark. She wants more colour. I've told you about 50 times. The P is pitch black, why is there no gold or chromatic shine on it? Add pop."

## What changed in the rules
- premium-restraint's 5 percent accent cap stays for FLAT COLOUR. For chrome gold and chrome platinum it is lifted: metal may be a real part of the screen.
- Glass stays 100 percent see-through (clear-glass-spec): no fills, no tinted slabs.
- Gold stays the pale low-chroma chrome gold (#fef9e8 / #f0e6c7 / #a4845d / #332814). Never yellow, mustard, amber or orange.

## Where the metal goes
1. THE P (hero): chrome, environment reflection, moving specular sweep, rim light, leans with the pointer. Never black.
2. Key headline ("JOIN THE PEPTICITY TEAM"): platinum chrome with a shimmer of darkness running through it. No panel or rectangle behind it. Centred on the page axis.
3. The two corner ticks (top left, top right): lit metal.
4. Edges, rails, rules, tile borders, product frames.
5. Mix both metals: gold version gets platinum on some type and tiles, platinum version gets gold touches.
6. The membership card: centred, a more distinctive silhouette (cut or notched corners, metal hairline), a glint that follows the pointer.

## Light
- Ground lifted from near-black to deep graphite or smoke. A viewer must not say "dark".
- Add light, not just colour: soft metal light pools behind glass, never a coloured backdrop.

## Proof (numbers, in a real browser)
- Mean hero luminance before and after (target mean L* of at least 35).
- Sampled P pixels are not black.
- Accent/metal coverage is reported, not capped.
- Plus the hover-everything audit.
