# WORDING RULES: CHECKLIST AND BANNED PHRASES

Run this over every PEPTICITY word before it ships: pages, product slugs, meta descriptions, alt text, image text, schema, ads, social captions, emails (subject lines too), bot replies, quiz and result copy, checkout, receipts, and anything an affiliate or influencer posts for PEPTICITY. Risk scan, not legal advice. Builds on S0 section 6 and the `regulated-claims-check` skill.

**The core finding:** hedged research framing is not a shield; it is the evidence. "Studied for", "investigated for" and "may support" establish intended use [S70, S25]. A disclaimer does not cure the sentence. **Delete the sentence and write the factual line instead.**

## A. PRE-PUBLISH CHECKLIST (every item must be "yes")
**Products and claims**
- [ ] No product name sits beside a goal, benefit, body part, condition or symptom (same sentence, card, heading, image or page section).
- [ ] No unauthorized product is named or priced publicly (names hidden until counsel clears).
- [ ] Any authorized Rx product shown publicly shows only name, price and quantity, on a neutral list.
- [ ] No outcome, benefit or "may help" language for any product, however hedged.
- [ ] No sameness: never "same as", "generic", "equivalent to", "like Ozempic", unless it is a Health Canada-authorized generic named correctly.
- [ ] No borrowed authority: no "clinically proven/studied", "doctor recommended", "FDA/Health Canada approved" (unless true for that exact product and use, with the DIN), "FDA-approved pharmacy", "pharmaceutical grade".
- [ ] No trial figures, percentages or study summaries next to a product.
- [ ] No before/after images, no body-transformation imagery, no lab-coat or stethoscope staging that implies a clinic.

**Use instructions in disguise**
- [ ] No doses, units, schedules, cycles, stacks, protocols, mixing, reconstitution volumes, injection sites or techniques, calculators, "how to use" guides.
- [ ] No diluent, syringes, needles, alcohol swabs or "kits" sold or linked beside any peptide.
- [ ] Product templates have no "dosage", "cycle" or "protocol" fields (delete the fields, don't leave them empty).

**People and proof**
- [ ] No invented stats, testimonials, reviews, star ratings, client counts or clinician names. Placeholders are clearly marked `[Your verification process]`.
- [ ] No testimonials mentioning effects. Affiliates and influencers get written rules and are monitored.
- [ ] Clinician claims are verifiable (licence and province) and use real, consented names only.

**Price and pressure**
- [ ] Every price is all-in (consult, pharmacy fee, shipping) or the mandatory extras are shown in the same place, same size. Taxes noted.
- [ ] Auto-renewal and its price shown beside the button; cancellation route stated and easy.
- [ ] What isn't charged if the clinician says no is stated (confirm with Grace).
- [ ] No countdown timers, "only N spots left", fake scarcity or urgency on a medical service.

**Consent, privacy, age**
- [ ] Marketing consent boxes are separate and unticked (CASL).
- [ ] Health answers have express, up-front consent wording (PIPEDA; Law 25 in Québec).
- [ ] No quiz or bot health text reaches ad pixels, analytics or session replay.
- [ ] Adults only; provincial minimum (18 or 19) enforced with a kind hard stop.

**The bot**
- [ ] Named "PEPTICITY GUIDE"; never Dr., Nurse, MD, NP, "your clinician" as its own voice; no white coat or stethoscope avatar (AB 489).
- [ ] Says it is not a clinician and not medical advice, in the header and first message.
- [ ] Emergency check runs before anything else: 911, and 9-8-8 (Canada) / 988 (US) for crisis.
- [ ] Every refusal ends with a next step (quiz, book, person).
- [ ] No therapy or emotional-support role (Illinois, Nevada, Utah laws).

**Record**
- [ ] Screenshot and date-stamp the published state.

## B. BANNED PHRASES (audit list)
Matching is by meaning, not exact words. Variants, plurals and translations count.

**1. Outcome language (however hedged)**
heals, healing, repairs, regenerates, cures, treats, reverses, prevents, restores, rejuvenates, boosts, burns, melts, shreds, torches, sculpts, tones, tightens, firms, optimizes, enhances, improves, supports, promotes, balances, detoxes, cleanses, activates, unlocks, resets, fat loss, weight loss (beside a product), lose X lb/kg, faster recovery, injury recovery, tendon/joint/gut repair, muscle growth, lean mass, more energy, better sleep, focus, mood, anti-anxiety, libido, arousal, performance, stamina, longevity, anti-aging, youth, age reversal, telomere lengthening, collagen boost, glow (as a claim), skin whitening/lightening/brightening, tanning, immune boost, may help, can help, helps with, designed to, formulated for, known for, popular for.

**2. Research framing used as a claim**
studied for, researched for, investigated for, explored for, shown to, linked to, associated with, evidence suggests, early research shows, in animal studies, in clinical studies, science-backed, research-backed, evidence-based (about a product), "for research purposes" next to human-use cues.

**3. Use instructions in disguise**
dose, dosing, dosage, mg/mcg/IU per, units, protocol, cycle, on/off cycle, stack, stacking, loading phase, titrate, taper, microdose, reconstitute, reconstitution, mix with, bac water ratio, syringe, insulin syringe, pin, pinning, subQ, sub-Q, IM, inject (as instruction), injection site, calculator, "how to use", "how to take", "beginner guide", "starter kit", "bundle with bac water".

**4. Sameness and authority**
same as Ozempic / Wegovy / Mounjaro / Zepbound, same active ingredient, generic Ozempic or Mounjaro (unless a correctly named authorized generic), equivalent, identical, copycat, "like the prescription", clinically proven, clinically studied, clinically tested, doctor recommended, doctor formulated, physician grade, pharmaceutical grade, medical grade, research grade, lab grade, Health Canada approved / FDA approved (unless true for that product and use, with DIN), FDA-registered, FDA-approved pharmacy, GMP certified (unless verified and relevant), 99% purity (as a selling claim), "research use only", "not for human consumption" (alongside any human-use cue), "for laboratory use".

**5. Borrowed authority in the bot or brand**
Dr., Doctor, Nurse, MD, NP, PA, RN, pharmacist (as the bot), "your clinician" (in the bot's own voice), "medical team" (if no team exists), white coat, stethoscope, clinic imagery that implies the bot is staff, "diagnosis", "assessment" (for the quiz; use "quiz" or "check-in").

**6. Pricing and pressure**
"from $X" when mandatory fees aren't included, "free" when anything mandatory costs money, hidden auto-renewal, lock-in not shown, countdown timers, "only N spots left", "limited stock", "selling fast", "today only", fake reviews, star ratings without a real source, before/after images, "guaranteed results", "money-back guarantee" (unless real, written and confirmed with Grace).

**7. Product names that are claims**
"Wolverine", "GLOW", "Body Protection Compound" as a selling line, "Barbie drug", "fat-burning peptide", "healing peptide", "the youth peptide", "the female Viagra". Counsel to review blend and product names.

## C. SAFE REPLACEMENTS (use these patterns)
| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
| "[Product] helps you recover faster" | "Your focus: TRAINING AND RECOVERY. Next step: a consult with a clinician licensed in [province]." |
| "Clinically proven peptides" | "A licensed clinician decides if a plan is right for you." |
| "Same active ingredient as Mounjaro" | "Your clinician can explain every option, including Health Canada-authorized ones." |
| "Pharmaceutical-grade, 99% pure" | "Prepared by a licensed partner pharmacy. [Testing documentation: confirm with Grace]" |
| "From $99/month" (fees extra) | "Consult $[xx] CAD. Programs from $[xx] CAD for [quantity], including [pharmacy fee, shipping]. Taxes extra." |
| "Dosing guide" | Remove. "Instructions come with your plan from the pharmacy." |
| "Research use only" | Remove. "Nothing is sold for research use. Every plan is prescribed by a licensed clinician." |
| "Dr. Pep, your peptide expert" | "PEPTICITY GUIDE · Not medical advice" |

## D. WHERE THE LIST IS ENFORCED IN CODE
`pepticity/shared/pep-brain.js` `BANNED` regex is the bot's last line of defence. When this list changes, update `BANNED` and `MED` there and check `PEPBRAIN.libBad` is empty. (`pep-brain.js` refers to a test suite at `tools/brain-test.js`; it is not in the repo as of 2026-10-26, so locate or rebuild it.) The regex is narrower than this list on purpose (to avoid false positives in vetted lines); the human audit uses this full list.
