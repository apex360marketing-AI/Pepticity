## 1. FDA BASICS
- **New drug approval (FD&C Act s.505)** and **misbranding (s.502)**: a product marketed for human use without approval is an "unapproved new drug".
- **Intended use** comes from the seller's words and context, not the label's disclaimer [VERIFY 21 CFR 201.128 citation]. FDA's 2026-08-24 letters: "despite statements on your product labeling", the websites' content "establishes that your products are intended to be drugs for human use" [S25].
- **Evidence FDA used in 2026:** dosing information, injection instructions, dose calculators, a "peptide guide", bacteriostatic water sold alongside, and claims about weight loss, metabolic regulation, mitochondrial function, sexual dysfunction and growth-hormone signalling [S25].
- **Royal Peptides LLC (2026-08-24):** named "Tirzepatide", "Semaglutide", "Retatrutide", "SS-31 (Elamipretide)", "PT-141", "Tesamorelin" and "BIMORELIN" as unapproved new drugs [S25]. Other recipients reported: Peak Performance Peptides, NuScience Peptides, Peptide Partners, TXP Innovations (Tex Peptides) [S25]. Earlier: Gram Peptides (2026-03-31) [S25].
- FDA's letters did not confirm the products even contained what the labels said [S25].

## 2. COMPOUNDING: 503A AND 503B
- **503A** (state-licensed pharmacy, patient-specific prescription) may use bulk ingredients that have a USP monograph, are components of an approved drug, or are on the 503A bulks list; Category 1 substances are under evaluation and may be used meanwhile; **Category 2 = "significant safety concerns", not to be compounded** [VERIFY exact framework wording]. 503A pharmacies may not regularly compound "essentially copies" of commercially available drugs.
- **503B** outsourcing facilities: larger batches, CGMP, their own bulks and shortage rules.

## 4. FTC
- **Substantiation:** the Health Products Compliance Guidance (Dec 2022) says health benefit claims generally need randomized, controlled human trials; animal, in-vitro and anecdotal evidence does not count [S60].
- **FTC v. NextMed (final 2025-12-03):** $150,000; advertised prices left out drug, labs and consult; hidden 12-month lock-in; obstructed cancellation; fake reviews [S59].
- **Consumer Reviews and Testimonials Rule** (final 2024-08-14, effective 2024-10-21): bans fake reviews and testimonials (including AI-generated), buying reviews, and fake social indicators; civil penalties for knowing violations [S61].
- **Negative option / click-to-cancel rule** was vacated by the Eighth Circuit on 2025-07-08, but ROSCA and Section 5 still apply to auto-renewal and cancellation [S62].
- **6(b) orders on AI chatbots (2025-09-11)**: study of testing, age limits and risk disclosure for companion bots [S0].
- **Health Breach Notification Rule** applies to many health apps not covered by HIPAA [VERIFY current scope].
