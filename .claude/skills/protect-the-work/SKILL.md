# PROTECT THE WORK

The owner's words (4 Nov 2026): "every time I get close, you ruin it ... you deleted everything that was good about it ... make you take your time and really make sure you watch what we're doing. You follow the brand. You don't just go willy-nilly."

## 1. START FROM WHAT HE LIKES, NEVER FROM BLANK
- The base is the newest site he approved: sites/main/v5 (live /site-one/). Copy it into a new folder and change pieces there. Never write a new site from an empty file.
- Keep everything he liked unless he says to drop it: the chest film, the 3D P hero, backgrounds, tour, films, the GRACE'S LETTER lettering, the centred PEPTICITY footer.
- Never cover a film or hero image with new text or forms. Never put a form over the entrance film.
- Before deleting or replacing ANY existing piece, list it to the owner and wait for his yes.

## 2. USE THE REAL THINGS, NEVER COPIES
- Headings and logo: the real 3D type engine (brand/font/type3d-engine.js, PEPTICITY STUDIO look: matte gunmetal faces, UV-black sides, hairline light edge 0.68). CSS gradient text is NOT allowed as a stand-in.
- Logo P = the font's P with the cut-out back, in that 3D finish (CLAUDE.md). The old p-mark is retired.
- Effects, buttons, cards, hovers, menus: the built modules in apex_agents_bots/design/library and the v4/v5 code. If a picked effect has no module yet, say so; do not write a cheap look-alike.
- Imagery and film: Higgsfield only (credit-guard). Never a still of a film used as wallpaper under new text.

## 3. BRAND (check before and after every change)
- Colour: the current site colour is CHROME SILVER on near-black (owner's BLACK image: wall #323232, metal #080807 to #8E8D85) with one sparing accent. Any colour change (e.g. chrome gold + matte black, owner leaning 4 Nov) is shown as SAMPLES first and applied only after he picks.
- Shapes: NO squares, NO plain rectangles, NO pills, NO circles as containers. Cut corners (PEPTICITY clip-path), radius at most 3px, liquid glass / glassmorphism / neumorphism like APEX DESK. Glass is fully see-through: no fill, colour only on edges.
- Text must be readable on its background (contrast AA). No black on black. No text that starts dimmed.
- No moving ticker bands with symbols. No sliced-up product photos. No "which shelf first" style plain quiz boxes.
- Spacing: no pinned section holds longer than its content; measure gaps (max ~1 screen of empty space anywhere).
- Headings in CAPITALS; PEPTICITY always in capitals.

## 4. SLOW DOWN: ONE PIECE, PROVEN
1. Re-read the owner's last 3 messages, CLAUDE.md and this skill. Write his asks as a numbered list in his words.
2. Build ONE piece (one section, one still, one film).
3. Screenshot at 1440 and 390. Look at every screenshot.
4. Write a pass/fail line for EVERY rule in sections 1-3 against the screenshots. Any fail = fix before showing.
5. Show the picture + link + the pass/fail list. Wait for his answer before the next piece.

pepticity-brand-lock, premium-restraint, clear-glass-spec, liquid-glass-studio, apex-glassmorphism, holo-glass-ui, hologram-panels, luxury-3d-logo, 4d-direction, cinematic-scroll, gsap-scroll-choreography, cinematic-pull-apart-scroll, effect-picks-enforcer, premium-micro-interactions, anti-slop-gate, brand-consistency-gate, verify-before-done, plus Superpowers (brainstorming, writing-plans, verification-before-completion). Name the ones used in the report.

## 6. NEVER
- Never say "done" without the pass/fail list.
- Never touch v5, the live pages or gh-pages without the owner's yes.
- Never decide a colour, logo, font or layout change alone.
