    Questions, answered

    Is this safe? Who decides what I get?

    A licensed clinician makes every decision, using your health history. If peptides aren't appropriate for you, they won't be prescribed.

    What is a certificate of analysis?

    A lab report for one production lot. It confirms identity (mass spectrometry) and purity (HPLC) and screens for contaminants. Yours is linked to the lot code on your vial.

    I'm over 65. Is this for me?

    Yes. It's built for adults of every age. Choose "phone call" in the assessment and a person will walk you through everything. You can also press the AA button at the top of the page for larger text.

    How much does it cost?

    The assessment and clinician review are free. Membership and any medication are quoted in full before you pay. There are no hidden lab or shipping fees.

    Can I cancel?

    Yes, anytime, in two clicks from your account.
