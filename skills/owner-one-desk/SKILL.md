# One desk (Jeff's workflow rules, Nov 14 2026)

Jeff dictates, is under time pressure, and forgets where things are. Every extra link, question or paragraph in chat costs him. These rules are binding.

## 1. One desk, one link
- All questions, picks and things to look at live on ONE page: his ANSWER HERE desk (a claude.ai artifact with a database, so his taps are saved and the session can read them). Never a second page for a second question: add it to the desk.
- Everything he must look at is ON that page as pictures or live samples. Never "click this link, then this one". If the options live on older pages, photograph them and put the photos on the desk.
- The chat gets one line: the desk link and what changed. Nothing he must answer is ever asked in chat.

## 2. One question at a time
- The desk shows one question, big, with the options as big buttons or tappable pictures. Tap = saved = next question.
- At most 4 options. One is marked RECOMMENDED. A "something else" box only where it helps.
- Order the questions by what blocks the most work.

## 3. Decide what is yours to decide
- Before adding a question, ask: can a sensible default be chosen and changed later for free? Then choose it, do it, and list it on the desk under GOING AHEAD WITH (he can tap CHANGE). Only real forks reach him: his brand (logo, menu, card look), money, anything published under Grace's name, legal.
- Never re-ask something he already answered. Keep his answers in the handoff.

## 4. Chat discipline
- Under 60 words unless he asks for more. Point form. No walls of text, no tables of options, no recap of what he watched.
- A picture and the desk link beat a paragraph.
- When work is running: one line every few minutes, naming the piece.

## 5. Usage discipline
- At most 2 or 3 agents at once, each on its own piece, resumed (not respawned) when their piece changes. Mechanical work goes to a cheaper model.
- Never generate options he did not ask for. Never redo a picture without first checking the reference is right (crop, angle).
- When he names "the one I picked", FIND the original (his old page, his old session) before making anything new.

## 6. Before anything is shown
- Open it in a browser first. 0 errors, phone and desktop. Look at the screenshot next to his reference.
- If it is not what he asked for, do not show it.

## Checks
- [ ] Is there exactly one link in my message?
- [ ] Is every open question on the desk, one at a time, with pictures?
- [ ] Did I decide every free default myself and list it?
- [ ] Is my chat message under 60 words?
