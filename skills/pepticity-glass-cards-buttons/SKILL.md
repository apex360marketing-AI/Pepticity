# PEPTICITY glass cards and buttons (Jeff's rules, Nov 13-14 2026)

The reference for the material is his menu pick 58 (/home/user/work/menu58/m58.jpg): liquid chrome with blue streaks through it, a blue crystal for the active key, a clear dark glass panel with a thin glowing blue edge.

## Cards
- CLEAR glass: liquid glass / glassmorphism you can see the background through. Fill at most 6 to 10 percent, backdrop blur, a bright thin rim light, a wet highlight top-left. Sharp, not milky.
- No rectangles, no squares, no pills. Big angled cuts, notches and slashes. And NO POINTS: no arrow or chevron ends on cards or buttons ("you made it pointy... that's not what we do").
- A watermark of the P in the back of the card, about 2 to 4 percent, never fighting the text.
- Light bars are LENS shaped like the light through the logo's P: thickest and brightest in the middle, thinning to nothing well before the card's edges, with a soft glow. Never a hard line running edge to edge.
- The card SITS IN 3D: real depth (perspective, layered translateZ, a floor shadow and a blue under-glow), a few degrees of pointer tilt at most.
- Bright accents: platinum chrome lettering with the blue light, chrome gold only as a small accent.

## Buttons
- Chrome or glass keys with cut corners (no points, no pills, no plain rectangles), CAPITALS, no lazy words.
- Hover: the key comes OFF the screen toward the viewer (translateZ / scale 1.04 to 1.08 with a growing shadow), its text grows slightly, and light blooms BELOW it in the logo blue. Press: it seats back down. Focus: a bar of light at the foot.
- Reduced motion: no travel; the light change only.

## Never
- Flat white text or fills. Violet or purple. A second blue. Flashing, timers, red SALE.
- Heavy dark fills that hide the background. Edge-to-edge glowing lines.

## Before showing
- Check at 1440x900 and 390x844, hover and focus states, reduced motion, 0 page errors.
- Look at the screenshot yourself next to m58.jpg: does it belong to the same family?
