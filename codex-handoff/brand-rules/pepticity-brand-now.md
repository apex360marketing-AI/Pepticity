# PEPTICITY brand, as ruled (newest ruling wins)

Read the approved references before designing: `work/cardart/FOUNDERS-CARD-LOCKED-V1.png` (the member card he approved),
the Founders section in the review build (`work/r4/rc4`), menu render 58. Match them; do not invent a new look.

## The look
- GROUND: near-black (#0b0b0c), matte, quiet. Never white, never a coloured wash.
- GLASS: clear and see-through (backdrop blur + a faint cool sheen). It must read as glass, not a solid panel.
- EDGE: one thin bright-chrome edge with a thin black-chrome line inside it. NOTHING else round a card: no thick rims,
  no ripples or wavy light streaks, no smoke, no glow haze, no extra trim. He rejected that four times ("nice clean border").
- CORNERS: cut (chamfered). A card and anything shaped like it has all four corners cut. Never a pill, never fully
  rounded ends, never pointed ends, and no plain rectangles or boxes round fields.
- METAL: platinum chrome and black chrome with an ice-blue bevel for lettering. Chrome gold (pale, low colour) only as a small accent, mainly on the dashboards.
- ONE BLUE (#2a78ff, ice #cfe6ff at its hot centre), only as LIGHT. The signature is a THIN light line: thickest in
  the middle, tapering to nothing at both ends. He likes the light along the bottom of a card. Never a hard edge-to-edge bar.
- TYPE: PepticityBlack, CAPITALS for every heading, button and label. The brand is always PEPTICITY.
- A title that names the card uses the card's OWN lettering (same render), not a different chrome.

## Layout and words
- Symmetrical. Things that belong together sit level and centred on the same line. Measure it; do not eyeball.
- Almost no writing. Explanations go in drop-downs (one open at a time), never paragraphs.
- Forms: fields are open, on a light line; choices and buttons take the card's cut-corner shape. Always collect name,
  email AND phone, with separate unticked consent boxes. No health, dosing or goal wording.
- Founders: PEPTICITY PLATINUM FOUNDERS RESERVE. 25 numbered cards (NO. 08 of 25, lowercase "of"). Earned, not automatic:
  join, refer two friends, both sign up. All offers are 10%.

## Motion
- Buttons and menu keys visibly come OUT on hover (lift + grow + brighter light) and settle on press.
- Things are calm at rest and light up when touched (the PEPTIDES crystal is a calm blue until hovered).
- The P spins fast and stops dead, flat to the front. Reduced motion: no movement.
