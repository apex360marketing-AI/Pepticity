            The science

            Built on proof, not promises.

            This page explains how a plan is decided, who prepares it and how each lot is tested. It does not tell you what any peptide will do for you. Only your clinician can talk about that.

            01

            The clinician decides

            A licensed clinician reviews your answers, your medicines and, when needed, your labs. They decide if a plan is right for you. Nothing is sold without that review.

            02

            A licensed pharmacy prepares it

            Plans are filled by a licensed partner pharmacy [name and licence: confirm with Grace]. A compounded medicine is not the same as an FDA-approved one, and we never say it is.

            03

            An independent lab tests each lot

            Each lot is tested for identity, purity, sterility and endotoxin by [independent lab: confirm with Grace] before release.

            You can check all of it

            The lot code on your vial opens that lot's certificate. The pharmacy and clinician licences link to the boards that issued them.
