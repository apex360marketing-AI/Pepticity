How these were built

  • One brain. All 15 use the trained PEPTICITY guard and Q&A (emergency, under-18, medical and outcome questions go to a clinician or a person) plus a class-level knowledge layer.
  • One logo slot. The P comes from a single swap point (--logo-p in shared/gallery.css, file assets/p-mark.svg). The new P drops in there and every bot, 3D ones included, picks it up.
  • Real 3D. Bots 01 to 05, 09 and 13 to 15 are three.js lit objects with reflections. Nothing is hand-drawn.
  • Accessible. Every bot works with a keyboard, shows focus, and has a reduced-motion version.
  • Compliance switch. Add ?safe=1 to any bot address to see the version with no peptide families, no pairings and no clinician or assessment wording. Needs the owner's pick before publishing (see the report).
