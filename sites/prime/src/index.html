    A short guide you can read at your own pace. No jargon. If a word is new, we explain it.

    On this page

    What peptides are

    A peptide is a short chain of amino acids. Amino acids are the small building blocks that proteins are made of. Your body makes many peptides on its own.

    Some peptides are made in a lab and used as medicines. Others are used in cosmetics, like skin creams. A medicine and a cosmetic follow very different rules.

    How the rules work

    Approved medicines have been reviewed by the FDA (in the U.S.) or Health Canada. Compounded medicines are made by a licensed pharmacy for one person, from a prescription. Compounded medicines are not FDA-approved, and they are not the same as an approved drug.

    In both cases, you need a prescription from a licensed clinician. That is why every Pepticity Prime plan starts with one.

    Cosmetics are for the look and feel of skin. They do not need a prescription, and they must not claim to treat anything.

    How testing works

    Each batch made at one time is called a lot. An independent lab tests a sample from every lot. It checks that the product is what the label says, and how pure it is.

    The lab writes the results on a certificate of analysis. You or your doctor can ask us for it at any time.

    What to watch out for

    • Websites that sell peptides “for research use only” to the public.
    • Anyone who sells a prescription product without a clinician.
    • Big promises, like “turn back the clock.”
    • No phone number, and no way to see test results.

    Questions to ask your doctor

    1. Is this a good idea with the medicines I already take?
    2. Is it an approved medicine or a compounded one?
    3. What is known about it, and what is not known yet?
    4. Who makes it, and can I see the test results?
    5. How will we know if I should stop?
