How it works

SIX CHECKS BETWEEN THEIR REQUEST AND YOUR PHONE.

Every lead goes through the same six steps before it reaches you. Here is what happens at each one.

  1. Step 01 · Request

    THEY ASK FOR A QUOTE

    A real person in [City] fills in a request for your service: what they need, where, and when.

  2. Step 02 · Consent

    THEY AGREE TO HEAR FROM YOU

    Their consent to be contacted by your business specifically is recorded with a timestamp, so every call you make is on solid ground.

  3. Step 03 · Verify

    WE CHECK THEY'RE REAL

    Phone and email are checked. Fake names, test entries and duplicates are filtered out before you ever see them. [Your verification process]

  4. Step 04 · Qualify

    WE CHECK THEY'RE SERIOUS

    A few quick questions about budget, timing and the job itself. You see the answers, so the first call starts warm.

  5. Step 05 · Match

    MATCHED TO ONE BUSINESS

    The lead goes to the one business that owns that industry and territory. Exclusive: nobody else gets it. [Your exclusivity terms]

  6. Step 06 · Deliver

    IT LANDS ON YOUR PHONE

    Pushed into your CRM with an instant text and email alert. Track it, work it, and return it for credit if it is bad. Sample shown.

End of the dark token set. The same module follows below with the light tokens, no code changes.
