# VERSION 10 · CHROME PLATINUM: choices to approve or change

- NAV-04 Elevator Floors: wired the same way as version 06 (floor readout in the top bar, THE TOUR opens the brushed-metal panel). Panel metal is mid graphite-silver (#3C3F45 mixed with near-white), the only large metal area, and only while the menu is open.
- BTN-105 Convex Lens: used on the two main sign-up buttons (hero and SEND MY SHORTLIST). Label is the platinum mirror gradient (slow drift); edge is the platinum gradient hairline.
- CRD-119 Glass Slab: used on the text-alerts card. The module's "Turn over" back face is NOT used (it would need new copy). Only the front face, the tilt edge and the light band are used.
- SCR-104 Counter Columns: the six HOW WE CHOOSE steps are six tiles, 2 per column (columns 1 and 3 drift up, column 2 drifts down). The tile text is the same as before ("STEP 01 OF 06" label added from the step number). The HOW WE CHOOSE heading sits on the see-through plate. ASK GRACE button stays inside step 06.
- TXT-103 Tide Fill: same per-word method as versions 03 and 07. Key phrases (the marked words) get the mirror sheen, a glint delayed word by word, and an underline.
- HOV-104 Lead Trail: the module wants one section as its root, so a fixed see-through overlay is the root and the pointer over any glass surface (cards, menu, buttons, nav) is forwarded to it. Trail cards say "STOP 01 ... WELCOME" etc. using only the site's own stop names (no new copy). Mouse only.
- CRD-129 Viewfinder: placed on the BRING A FRIEND IN card (refer a friend), the one feature card whose job is "point at someone". The module's light leak is neutral white and only on hover. The eyebrow SHARE PEPTICITY carries the blinking record dot.
- BGD-102 plexus behind FOUR QUICK QUESTIONS was removed (it was a base slot module, not on the keep list). The card now sits on plain glass.
- Removed from the page: NAV-102, BGD-102, SCR-102, TXT-04 (statements), BTN-101, CRD-118 (links, scripts, mount list). The older TXT-04 scroll-highlight on small "sub" lines (data-hl) is the base's own and stays.
- Ground: neutral graphite lift (#222428) plus a soft white light at the top and a gentler vignette; the liquid smoke is the same animation, brightened with a CSS filter (brightness 1.4). Change that one number to taste.
- Glass fill everywhere on this page is at most 2% white; photo glass (city, lab) has no dark fill, the words get a soft shadow instead.
