The science, plainly.

What peptides are, how the two lanes are regulated, and how every lot is tested. No promises, just the process.

What a peptide is.

A peptide is a short chain of amino acids joined by peptide bonds. Proteins are long chains; peptides are short ones, often two to fifty amino acids. The order of the amino acids is called the sequence, and it defines the molecule.

GHK, used in our cosmetic shelf, is a tripeptide: glycine, histidine and lysine (glycyl-L-histidyl-L-lysine), about 340 daltons. It binds copper(II) ions, and the copper complex, listed on labels as Copper Tripeptide-1, gives solutions a faint blue tint. GHK was first described in human plasma in 1973.

Two lanes, regulated differently.

Prescription peptides are only available after a licensed clinician decides they're appropriate for you. A licensed partner pharmacy prepares them under its state or provincial licence.

Compounded medications are not FDA-approved or Health Canada-authorized products, and they are not equivalent to approved drugs.

The cosmetic shelf

Our topicals are cosmetics. In the US, cosmetics are regulated by the FDA under the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act and the Modernization of Cosmetics Regulation Act of 2022. In Canada, they fall under Health Canada's Cosmetic Regulations.

Cosmetics are not drugs. They aren't intended to diagnose, treat or prevent anything, so we only describe how skin looks and feels.

How each lot is tested.

Every lot gets a certificate of analysis from an independent lab before it can be released. You can open it with the code on your box.

  1. IdentityMass spectrometry confirms the molecule matches its written sequence. [method: confirm with lab]
  2. PurityHPLC measures how much of the sample is the intended peptide. Result printed on the certificate.
  3. ContaminantsScreens for microbes and heavy metals, and sterility and endotoxin where the pharmacy lane requires it.
  4. Cosmetic safetyFor the shelf: preservative efficacy and stability testing on the finished formula.
  5. ReleaseOnly lots that pass are released. The certificate is linked to the lot code.
