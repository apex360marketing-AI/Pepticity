§1

Abstract

Most peptide sellers ask you to trust a single certificate. We think you should be able to check every lot yourself, the good ones and the ones we held back. So every lot we sell, and every lot we refuse to sell, gets its full panel published: identity by mass spectrometry, purity by HPLC, endotoxin, heavy metals and microbial counts.

The care itself starts with people. You tell us what you want from your health; a licensed clinician reviews it and decides if a plan is right for you. If it is, a licensed partner pharmacy prepares it, and the lot code on your box opens the same panel you can read on this page. Prices are shown in full before checkout, with nothing added at the end.

Read a lot like a chemist.

Scroll to run the sample, then drag across either trace. Every peak and every ion opens its numbers.

    Retention time. When each substance leaves the column, in minutes.

  2. ii

    Area %. How much of the sample is the main peak. Spec here: ≥ 98.0%.

  3. iii

    m/z. The mass the spectrometer sees. It confirms the molecule is the one on the label.

        04

        FAQ Questions & contact

        Ask anything. We answer in writing.

        Q1 What does “verify before you buy” mean?

        Every lot has a code. Enter it in the lot explorer and you see the full test panel: identity, purity, endotoxin, heavy metals and microbial results. You can do this before you order, and again when your box arrives.

        Q2 Who decides whether I get a plan?

        A licensed clinician decides if a plan is right for you. If they decide it isn’t, you are not charged for a plan. If prescribed, a licensed partner pharmacy prepares it.

        Q3 Where do you offer clinician plans?

        In the states and provinces where our clinicians are licensed. The intake asks where you live first so the right clinician sees it. [service area: confirm with Grace]

        Q4 Is the no-consult range a medicine?

        No. The no-consult range is cosmetic topicals, for the appearance and feel of skin. They are not intended to diagnose, treat or prevent any condition.

        Q5 Why do you publish lots that failed?

        Because a list of only passes proves nothing. Held lots stay in the ledger with their numbers and the reason they were held.

        Q6 What is included in the price?

        The checkout shows every line: clinician review, plan if prescribed, shipping and tax. There is no membership fee and nothing is added at the last step.

        Q7 How is it shipped?

        Plans that need it ship cold-chain in insulated packaging with a temperature indicator. Topicals ship in standard packaging. [shipping details: confirm]

        Q8 Can I ask about dosing or medical use here?

        No. We don’t give medical advice on this site or through Ask the lab. Those conversations belong with your clinician inside a plan.
