    DO AND DO NOT

    The short list that keeps every page, post, label and email looking like the same brand.

    DO

    • Matte black or crisp white grounds. Black can carry a faint ultraviolet sheen as texture. White stays cool, never cream.
    • One metal per piece. Platinum chrome, or the pale chrome gold. Never both together.
    • See-through glass. The background must show through every card and button: a light frost and a thin chrome edge.
    • Cut corners. Chamfered corners on cards and buttons. Where a corner is round, 3px at most.
    • Capitals for every heading, set in PEPTICITY BLACK. PEPTICITY is always written in capitals.
    • One slow light sweep on hover, left to right, about one and a half seconds.
    • A small glow edge where something needs to stand out. Small is the point.

    DO NOT

    • No peach, pink, rose, salmon or blush.
    • No yellow, mustard, amber, champagne or dull gold. The only gold is the pale chrome gold in the palette.
    • No green of any kind, and no big blue or purple areas.
    • No orange or warm cast on whites, metals or photos.
    • No rounded capsule buttons, and nothing shaped like an ampoule.
    • No milky or solid cards. If you cannot see the background through it, it is not PEPTICITY glass.
    • No bounces, colour flips or neon floods on hover.
