PEPTICITY BLACK

Your BLACK lettering, drawn as a full typeface: every capital A to Z, the digits and the basics, on one grid, with the same cuts, corners and weight as your picture. Everything in 3D below is live: move the pointer and scroll.

01

MATCH CHECK

Left: your reference picture. Right: the word BLACK set in the new font and rendered live in 3D, same framing and light direction.

  • MATCHVery wide letters, 1.1 to 1.45 times wider than tall
  • MATCHOne stroke weight everywhere, about 0.19 of the letter height
  • MATCHB drawn with no stem: three bars, open counters, left ends cut on one 45 degree line, lower bowl a touch wider
  • MATCHL bar ends in a cut that runs parallel to the A leg
  • MATCHA is an open chevron, no crossbar, with a separate lighter bar split off inside the right leg
  • MATCHC ends are mirrored diagonal cuts; outer corners rounded, inner corners tight
  • MATCHK arm and leg leave the stem near mid height; tops and feet cut flat
  • MATCHBrushed gunmetal faces, raised off a dark matte wall with a soft shadow down and to the left
  • CLOSEReference faces are slightly warm grey; ours are kept neutral to stay on brand (no warm or gold cast)
