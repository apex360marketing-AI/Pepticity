PEPTICITYLOGO REVIEW · R127

THE P MARK

The logo is the P from the new PEPTICITY BLACK font: same stroke, the rounded outer corner and half-round counter of the B, flat-cut stem. Below it as a flat mark, in 3D metal, and as the spinning emblem with PEPTICITY raised around the ring.

01

FLAT MARK

The vector mark on dark and on white. Exported as brand/logo/p-mark.svg.

02

3D METAL

Real extruded metal with a UV-black side wall, a hairline of light on the edge and a soft shadow. Platinum is the main finish; the pale gold-chrome is shown as the accent option. Move the pointer to tilt it.

03

THE SPINNING EMBLEM

The P inside a ring with PEPTICITY raised around it in the font, turning slowly like a coin. Both faces read the right way round. Scroll speeds it up a little.
