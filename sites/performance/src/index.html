      2. 02Lot testing

      01 / Lab testing

      How your lab testing works

      1. STEP 1OrderWe send a requisition to a partner lab near you [lab network: confirm].
      2. STEP 2DrawA short blood draw at the lab. Some markers need a morning or fasting draw; we'll tell you which.
      3. STEP 3ResultsThe lab reports each marker with its own reference range.
      4. STEP 4ReviewA licensed clinician goes through them with you, then decides if a plan is right for you.

      02 / Lot testing

      How every lot is tested

      IDENTITY

      Confirms the compound is what the label says.

      POTENCY / PURITY

      Measures how much is present and what else is there.

      STERILITY

      Checks for microbial contamination where required.

      ENDOTOXIN

      Checks for bacterial endotoxins where required.

      Testing is done by [testing lab: confirm, with accreditation]. Results are published as a certificate of analysis for each lot number.

      03 / Regulation

      How regulation works

      Prescription required
      Prescription items are only dispensed after a licensed clinician, licensed in your state, decides a plan is right for you.
      Licensed partner pharmacy
      Prescriptions are filled by a state-licensed partner pharmacy. Compounding pharmacies prepare medications for an individual patient with a prescription.
      Not FDA-approved
      Compounded medications are not FDA-approved and are not the same as an approved drug.
      Telehealth rules
      Consults follow the telehealth rules of the state you live in.
