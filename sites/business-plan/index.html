05RISKS · THE HONEST ROW

Peptides carry the most upside and the most regulatory risk. Use the PEPTIDES PAUSED switch to see the downside.

CANADIAN REGULATION

Health Canada says unauthorized injectable peptides are illegal to sell and has seized product and won an injunction against a seller. Selling the clinician service, not the product, is the working approach. Counsel must confirm. HEALTH CANADA ADVISORY · INJUNCTION

SUPPLIER RISK

No maker has quoted a minimum order, price or dropship terms yet. Vitamin and protein makers hold their own Canadian site licences; peptide supply has no equivalent licensed route in this research. Use the slider "paid share" and the margin sliders to stress it.

AD-PLATFORM LIMITS

Paid ads for peptides may be restricted, so growth leans on organic: quiz, health hub, bot, letter. Set "paid share of leads" to 0% to see an organic-only plan. Vitamins, protein and creatine are easier to advertise (copy rules still apply).
