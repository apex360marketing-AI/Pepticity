YOUR PEPTICITYCONCIERGE

It greets every visitor, asks how it can help, finds products, quotes exact prices and closes with the 15% grand-opening offer. It never says what a product does, never gives dosing and never gives medical advice.

WHAT YOUR CONCIERGE DOES FOR PEPTICITY

  1. 01

    ANSWERS CUSTOMERS 24/7

    Products, prices, shipping and the offer, at any hour, in plain words. Every answer is written and checked in advance.

  2. 02

    RECOMMENDS PRODUCTS AND ADDS THEM TO THE CART

    Finds products by name or nickname, opens shelves by interest, suggests quantity breaks and adds to the cart in one tap.

  3. 03

    CAPTURES EMAILS AND PHONE NUMBERS, WITH CONSENT

    Always with an unticked box the customer ticks themselves. Every contact is tagged by interest, so each follow-up fits.

    ENERGYRECOVERY55+SKIN AND HAIRGYM

    RECOVERS ABANDONED CARTS

    Remembers the cart, welcomes the customer back to it, and can send the saved-cart email to people who agreed to hear from PEPTICITY.

  5. 05

    RESTOCK ALERTS

    When something sells out, customers join the queue and hear the moment the next lot lands.

  6. 06

    CONNECTS TO SCHEDULED AUTOMATIONS

    Ready to plug into the emails that run on their own:

    WELCOME EMAILSABANDONED-CART EMAILSRESTOCK EMAILSWEEKLY NEWSLETTERSREVIEW REQUESTS
  7. 07

    FINDS NEW PROSPECTS, WITHIN THE LAW

    Builds outreach lists for PEPTICITY. Canada's anti-spam law (CASL) comes first: nobody is emailed from a scraped address without their consent.

  8. 08

    REPORTS WHAT CUSTOMERS ASK

    A plain summary of the top questions, the most-searched products and requests for items PEPTICITY doesn't stock yet.
