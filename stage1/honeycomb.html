THE HONEYCOMB MEDALLION

No circle. An outer hexagon, PEPTICITY running round the outside along the hexagon's edges, an inner hexagon, and a honeycomb inside it under the new P. Every layer is built at a different height, so the rims, the letters and the P stand out from the plate. Each version has its own colour, its own lighting and its own movement.
